41st Conference on Design of Circuits and Integrated Systems DCIS 2026

Ver na agenda oficial

The conference on Design of Circuits and Integrated Systems (DCIS) is a well-established international meeting that provides opportunities to both researchers in the academia and professionals of industry to exchange new ideas and application experiences in the highly active fields of micro-and nano-electronic circuits and integrated systems. The 41st edition of the conference is organised by INESC-ID and Instituto Superior Técnico, with INESC-ID researcher Fábio Passos as General Chair, and will provide an excellent forum to present and discuss works on the emerging challenges offered by technology, in the areas of modeling, design, implementation and test of devices, circuits and systems.

Paper submissions are open until 17 May, for contributions in various topics, including but not limited to: circuits & systems, emerging technologies, design & EDA, applications & education and reliability, security & sustainability.

Fonte: Instituto de Engenharia de Sistemas e Computadores Investigação e Desenvolvimento. Horário, local exato e preço na página oficial.
