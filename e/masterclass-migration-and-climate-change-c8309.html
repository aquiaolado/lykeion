Masterclass: Migration and Climate Change

Ver na agenda oficial

How do we understand and respond to displacement in a changing climate and digital world?

On 5 November, Melissa Siegel (UNU-MERIT), Robert Oakes (UNU-EHS) and Bushra Ebadi (UNU-EGOV) bring together three perspectives on this question.

The session moves from understanding the evidence on climate-related migration to modelling future mobility and examining how digital infrastructures can contribute to displacement. Together they ask what this evidence means for policy and practice.

📅 5 November | 14:00–16:00 CET | Online 🔗 Learn more and register: https://unu.edu/merit/course/migration-and-climate-change
