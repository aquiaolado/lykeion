About Resilient Creativity Resilient Creativity presents creative intelligence as a field-tested capability for doing serious creative work under constraint – where resources are limited, institutions are fragmented, society is structurally divided, and expectations are often low. Drawing on practice and education from a post-conflict society still rebuilding, the talk offers a grounded lens on how creative decisions are made, how trust is built, and how meaning is produced when the system doesn’t help – and argues that resilient creativity is also the work of rebuilding the demand for quality and making outcomes matter again. It invites the Action to define “Artistic Intelligence” in a way that survives contact with reality.

__ Kenan Zekić is a Bosnia and Herzegovina-based designer, educator, and publishing creative director. In 2023, he founded the INDI+E Association (Innovation, Design + Education) – a small, action-driven association, to push a simple idea: creativity isn’t a lifestyle – it’s a survival skill, especially in high-constraint, post-conflict realities. After years in academia – including a tenured full professorship (2006-2021) – he stepped out by choice to return to the field without leaving education, through mentoring, teaching, and collaboration. His work moves across art and design fields, from publishing and public communication to cultural-heritage projects and community-based initiatives, grounded in resilient creativity – practical methods for doing serious work when the system doesn’t help.
