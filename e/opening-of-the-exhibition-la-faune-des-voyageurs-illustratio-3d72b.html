Opening of the exhibition “La faune des voyageurs. Illustrations of Animals in 16th-Century Printed Books

Ver na agenda oficial

The exhibition presents a selection of animal illustrations found in a variety of works of travel literature. The chosen iconography includes illuminations from 15th-century manuscripts, as well as engravings published in printed books during the 15th and 16th centuries. Travellers and chroniclers, real or imagined, such as Marco Polo, Jean de Mandeville, André Thevet, Hans Staden and Gândavo, among others, described various animal species from the territories they visited in their accounts or natural histories. At the same time, artists involved in book production, who had often never travelled or encountered these animals themselves, played a decisive role in presenting them to European audiences through their illustrations.

The exhibition aims to highlight and celebrate this artistic work, including that of the illuminator Maître d’Egerton (15th century) and the engraver Jean Cousin (16th century), as well as other anonymous or lesser-known artists who succeeded in conveying the wonder, surprise, fear and curiosity, but also the limitations, found in the texts of travel literature.

The exhibition will be on display from 20 November to 11 December 2026 .
