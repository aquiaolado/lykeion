Here you’ll find information about upcoming events that we’re hosting or participating in, as well as an overview of the events we’ve hosted or participated in in the past.

Sign up now to participate in the Wave Makers Workshop! This workshop is part of our Blue Skills Workshops series (Workshops da Academia Azul) and is open to all residents of Madeira aged 16-30. No experience in engineering required! Note: workshop is in Portuguese.

MARtístico, the ocean art competition organized by the MARE-Madeira Youth Ambassadors, is back for its second edition! Whether an individual, professional or school, submit your ocean art for a chance to have your art displayed at the MARtístico exhibition and win ocean experiences!

The Blue Stories Workshop (‘Workshop de Narrativas Azuis’), part of our Academia Azul workshop series for young people in Madeira, shared insights and practical methods to pursue ocean-positive career paths related to storytelling, education and communication.

We hosted monthly chats with our sciences and collaborators, open to all, to share ocean science and conservation with the community.

We hosted the 12th International Conference on Marine Bioinvasions in Funchal, welcoming almost 300 attendees for a week of discussions on all this NIS!
