This seminar will explore the connections that shape marine ecosystems, from cetaceans to phytoplankton. Drawing on her experience as a whale-watching guide, Jacqueline Joosten will introduce cetacean ecology and migration, highlighting the importance of polar phytoplankton as the foundation of the krill-based food web. She will then present her research on Antarctic phytoplankton and the POLAR BIOMAP project , which investigates climate-driven changes in phytoplankton communities.

Jacqueline Joosten is an MSc student in Marine Biology at the University of Algarve (UAlg), with a background in marine conservation and molecular biology. She also has experience as a whale-watching guide in the Azores. Her current research focuses on Antarctic phytoplankton and polar marine ecosystems.
