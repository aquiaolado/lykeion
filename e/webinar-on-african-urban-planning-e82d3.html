Webinar on African Urban Planning

Ver na agenda oficial

We are pleased to invite you to the research webinar on African Urban Planning Histories. The event will feature presentations and critical discussions drawing from the contributions of the special issue of Planning Perspectives (Vol 41.4) focused on African urban planning.

The webinar will feature live presentations from the contributing authors of the special issue, who will share their findings, insights, and methodologies while discussing emerging trends in African urban planning. Attendees will also have the opportunity to engage directly with the authors during a live question-and-answer period.

Participation in the webinar is free, but registration is required. Register at https://forms.gle/BWM6VFyDg8Nj7khM9

• Date: 19 November 2026 (Thursday) • Time: 12.00 – 15:00, UTC (see UTC time at https://time.is/UTC] • Platform: Zoom

More information: https://sites.google.com/site/africanurbanplanning/webinars Full Program (PDF): https://drive.google.com/file/d/1ORHlWx4MrpRZfH8Zl8u7GqTZSjXVf3Py/view
