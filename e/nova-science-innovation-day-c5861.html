NOVA Science & Innovation Day

Ver na agenda oficial

NOVA University Lisbon is once again bringing together researchers, lecturers, students and partners for the NOVA Science & Innovation Day, which will take place on 18 November 2026 at the NOVA Rectorate. Throughout the day, the event will showcase research projects and their findings, initiatives in innovation and value creation, and the impact that the knowledge generated at NOVA has on society.
