III International Conference of Young Historians in Education: Submissions Open

Ver na agenda oficial

HISTEDUP and the IE-UL Young Researchers Forum are organising, on 23 November 2026 at the Institute of Education of the University of Lisbon, the III International Conference of Young Historians in Education. The event aims to give visibility to research in the History of Education and to strengthen the network of young researchers in the field.

Proposals must include a title, 3 to 5 keywords and an abstract of up to 300 words, with a maximum of two authors per proposal. They must be submitted in two files (one with the title and abstract, the other with the authors’ details) to This email address is being protected from spambots. You need JavaScript enabled to view it. by 25 October . Notification of acceptance will be given on 5 November.
