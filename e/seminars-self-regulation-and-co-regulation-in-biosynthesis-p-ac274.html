Seminars: Self-Regulation and Co-Regulation in Biosynthesis: Practices for Music Educators

Ver na agenda oficial

How can we be fully present and act with integrity in our relationships with students? This workshop offers a physical, auditory, and relational experience of Biosynthesis, designed for music educators. Through simple practices of grounding, centering, listening, and touch, we will explore how we regulate ourselves, how we regulate our interactions with others, and how this is reflected in the classroom. Aimed at the NOVA FCSH/CESEM community: master’s students, former master’s students, and faculty members.

Organized by: Portuguese School of Biosynthesis Facilitators: Catarina Lourenço de Carvalho and Rita Ferreira

Catarina Carvalho is a Body Psychotherapist and Junior Trainer in Body Psychotherapy (Biosynthesis). She holds a bachelor’s degree in Social and Organizational Psychology and a master’s degree in Music Therapy, with additional training in Psychosomatics, Epigenetics, Trauma, and Neuroscience. Catarina is a clinical supervisor.

Rita Ferreira is a Psychotherapist and Junior Trainer in Biosynthesis, with training in Psychosomatics, Epigenetics, Body Psychotherapy, and Transpersonal Psychotherapy. She holds a bachelor’s degree in Zootechnical Engineering and a postgraduate degree in Engineering and Technology Management. Rita is a member of the European Association for Psychotherapy.
