AI Horizon Conference 2026

Ver na agenda oficial

On November 11, Lisbon will host the AI Horizon Conference 2026 , organized by the AI Ethics and Integrity International Association (AIEI) . The conference brings together experts, entrepreneurs, investors, and professionals from different sectors to discuss the challenges and opportunities of Artificial Intelligence, with a focus on ethics, innovation, and responsible AI.

NOVA IMS is pleased to partner with this initiative, with Professor Miguel de Castro Neto, Dean of NOVA IMS, joining as a speaker to contribute to discussions on the development and responsible adoption of Artificial Intelligence.

The program features keynote talks, panel discussions, startup pitches, and networking opportunities, encouraging the exchange of knowledge and experiences among professionals working in technology, business, law, and public policy.

Attendance at the AI Horizon Conference 2026 is free, subject to organizer approval and availability. Premium tickets are also available.

This event will take place in the Lumen Hotel & The Lisbon Light Show , in Lisbon.

Fonte: NOVA IMS. Horário, local exato e preço na página oficial.
