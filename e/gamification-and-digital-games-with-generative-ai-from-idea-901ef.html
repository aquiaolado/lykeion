Gamification and Digital Games with Generative AI: From Idea to Practice Workshop

Ver na agenda oficial

How can a pedagogical idea be transformed into a digital game with the support of generative artificial intelligence (GenAI)? This workshop offers a theoretical and practical experience for university students and teachers interested in exploring this possibility. It will examine the foundations of gamification and game-based learning, linking learning objectives to narratives, challenges, rules, progression and feedback. In the practical session, participants will use GenAI to support the planning and creation of an educational digital game prototype. The process will involve developing and refining prompts, defining game mechanics, experimenting with the prototype and reviewing the results generated. Consideration will also be given to content accuracy, accessibility and the responsible use of GenAI. By the end of the workshop, participants are expected to understand the different stages involved in creating digital games with GenAI and to be able to assess the coherence between the game developed and its pedagogical purpose. This event is part of the first session of the workshop series "Emerging and Evolving Digital Technologies", organised by LabDERE/CIIE. Date: 20 November 2026 Time: 2 pm–6 pm Venue: Room 255, LabDERE/CIIE, Faculty of Psychology and Education Sciences, University of Porto (FPCEUP), Porto. Duration: 4 hours. Requirements for the practical session: A computer with internet access and access to a GenAI tool. Registration is now open! Click here to register. Short biography Anderson da Mota Moreira holds a Master's degree in Physics Education and teaches on undergraduate Education (Pedagogy) courses. He is currently pursuing a PhD in Education through the Northeast Network Graduate Programme (RENOEN), based at the Federal Rural University of Pernambuco (UFRPE). He is undertaking a period of doctoral research at the University of Porto, Portugal. His research investigates teachers' digital competences and skills related to the pedagogical use of generative artificial intelligence, with a focus on the development and validation of descriptors.
