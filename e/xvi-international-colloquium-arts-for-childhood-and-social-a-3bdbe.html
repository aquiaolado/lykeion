XVI International Colloquium Arts for Childhood and Social and Human Development

Ver na agenda oficial

A meeting that is a space for sharing ideas without borders between academic knowledge and artistic creation, between education and culture. Let’s join the voices of the ones that are passionate about the significance of the arts for education, teacher training and related research.

Venue: Fernando Rosado Room, Rua Bento de Jesus Caraça, 9A, 1495-686 Cruz Quebrada

To take part, please send an email to cesem@fcsh.unl.pt (with the subject line: “XVI EIAIDSH – Professor Christopher Azzara”)

Venue: Laboratory of Music and Communication in Childhood – Laboratory of Scenic Experimentation (Room 204, 2nd floor, CAN)

For students of the Department of Music Sciences – NOVA FCSH (Postgraduate, Master’s and PhD)

Fonte: Centro de Estudos em Música. Horário, local exato e preço na página oficial.
